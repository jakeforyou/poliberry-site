        How this works. Our forecast is what we expected for each county and state, locked on Nov 2. A county or state is on our forecast when it is within 2 points of it. A county counts once half its vote is in. A state uses its key counties until 90 percent of its vote is in, then its own count. Georgia, Ohio, Texas and North Carolina have their own rules, shown when you tap the state. Mirage and early-vote warnings sit beside a reading and never change it. Nothing on this tab changes a forecast. To see where a number came from, click it, or alt-click a state tile.
